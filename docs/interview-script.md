# Mock Simplified-Naturalization Interview Script

This is the **quality bar** for Hungarian Lingo's substance: a full mock
interview in the shape of the simplified naturalization
(*egyszerűsített honosítás*) conversation. The interview is not a formal exam —
it is a conversation with an official, conducted entirely in Hungarian, that
tests whether the applicant can genuinely communicate. The app must prepare a
learner to understand **every question below** and produce an adequate answer.

Critics judge coverage question-by-question: for each one, does the app teach
(a) the comprehension side — the question's key words and formal phrasing —
and (b) a production chunk the learner can adapt into their own answer?

Officials use formal address (*Ön*) throughout. Questions vary in wording
between offices; the app should teach chunks robust to paraphrase, so each
question lists common variants where they matter.

---

## 1. Érkezés és udvariasság — Arrival & courtesies

| # | Hungarian | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Jó napot kívánok! Foglaljon helyet! | Good day! Please take a seat. |
| 1.2 | Hogy van? | How are you? |
| 1.3 | Beszél magyarul? | Do you speak Hungarian? |
| 1.4 | Érti, amit mondok? | Do you understand what I'm saying? |
| 1.5 | Kérem a személyes iratait. | Your personal documents, please. |

The learner must also be able to say: *Elnézést, nem értem. Tudná ismételni?
Kérem, mondja lassabban.* (Sorry, I don't understand. Could you repeat that?
Please speak more slowly.) — the interview survival kit.

| # | Hungarian | English |
|---|---|---|

## 4. Származás — Hungarian ancestry

The heart of the simplified procedure: the applicant claims descent from a
Hungarian citizen.

| # | Hungarian | English |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Ki volt a magyar felmenője? | Who was your Hungarian ancestor? |
| 4.2 | Hogy hívták a nagymamáját / nagypapáját? | What was your grandmother's / grandfather's name? |
| 4.3 | Hol született a nagymamája? Melyik városban? | Where was your grandmother born? Which town? |
| 4.4 | Mikor vándorolt ki a családja? Miért? | When did your family emigrate? Why? |
| 4.5 | Beszéltek otthon magyarul? | Did you speak Hungarian at home? |
| 4.6 | Mit tud a magyar felmenőiről mesélni? | What can you tell me about your Hungarian ancestors? |

## 5. Mindennapok — Everyday life

| # | Hungarian | English |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Meséljen egy kicsit magáról! | Tell me a little about yourself. |
| 5.2 | Mit csinál egy átlagos napon? | What do you do on an average day? |

## 6. Magyarország és a magyarság — Hungary & Hungarian identity

| # | Hungarian | English |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Miért szeretne magyar állampolgár lenni? | Why do you want to be a Hungarian citizen? |
| 6.2 | Járt már Magyarországon? Hol? | Have you been to Hungary? Where? |
| 6.3 | Mit szeret Magyarországban? | What do you like about Hungary? |
| 6.4 | Tartja a kapcsolatot magyarországi rokonokkal? | Are you in touch with relatives in Hungary? |
| 6.5 | Milyen magyar ünnepeket ismer? Hogyan ünnepelnek? | What Hungarian holidays do you know? How do you celebrate? |
| 6.6 | Milyen magyar hagyományokat őriz a családja? | What Hungarian traditions does your family keep? |
| 6.7 | Mikor van a magyar nemzeti ünnep? | When are the Hungarian national holidays? (Mar 15, Aug 20, Oct 23) |

## 7. Lezárás — Closing

| # | Hungarian | English |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | Van kérdése? | Do you have any questions? |
| 7.2 | Köszönöm, végeztünk. Minden jót kívánok! | Thank you, we're done. All the best! |
| 7.3 | Az eskü szövegét ismeri? | Do you know the text of the oath? (awareness that an oath follows approval) |

## Coverage rule for critics

An interview question counts as **covered** only if the app teaches:

1. **Comprehension** — the question appears in a listening/reading exercise
   (exact or close variant), with its key vocabulary drilled.
2. **Production** — at least one answer chunk with personalization slots
   (e.g. *___-ban születtem*, *A nagymamám ___-ból származik*) is drilled in
   an active exercise (tile-building, dictation, or speaking), not only shown.
3. **Robustness** — where the table lists phrasing variants, at least the two
   most common variants are taught as the same intent.

Score = covered questions / 41. The bar is 41/41.
